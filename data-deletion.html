Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 13, 2026

JC Finds Publisher only keeps the ID of Instagram comments that asked for a link with the word «LINK», so we never reply twice. It is deleted automatically after 90 days. We do not keep names, messages or any other personal data.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Send an email to blacklinestyle.shop@gmail.com with the subject «Data deletion request».
  2. Include your Instagram or Facebook username so we can find any comment ID linked to it.
  3. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

You can also remove our app's access at any time from your Facebook or Instagram settings (Settings → Apps and Websites).
