Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

JC Finds ("we", "us") shares hand-picked product deals and trending finds on our social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest and TikTok). This policy explains how our publishing tool, JC Finds Publisher, handles information.

1. Information we collect

JC Finds Publisher connects only to our own business accounts to publish content and read performance statistics for our own posts (such as views, saves and clicks).

Comment replies («Comment LINK»). When someone comments the word «LINK» on one of our Instagram posts, our tool reads that comment (the comment text, the comment ID and the public username) only to send that person one private message with the product link they asked for, and to reply publicly that the link was sent. We keep only the comment ID, so we never reply twice to the same comment, and we delete it after 90 days. We do not use this information for advertising, we do not build profiles, and we never sell or share it.

2. Affiliate links

Some of our posts contain affiliate links (for example Amazon Associates, eBay Partner Network or other programs). When you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Retailers may use cookies according to their own privacy policies.

3. Third-party platforms

Our content is published on platforms such as Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Data retention and deletion

We do not store personal data from users beyond the comment IDs described above (deleted after 90 days). Access tokens for our own accounts are kept securely and can be revoked at any time. To ask us to delete any information related to you, see our data deletion instructions.

5. Children

Our content is not directed to children under 13.
